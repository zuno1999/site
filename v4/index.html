Pietro Zunino

Research Fellow in Power Systems & Low-Voltage EV Integration
“Studying the impact of electric vehicles on low-voltage distribution grids, developing strategies to transform EVs from a grid burden into a flexibility asset.”

I am a Ph.D. researcher investigating low-voltage grid hosting capacity, decentralized charging coordination, and ancillary grid services within the DTEC GREAT and EV4EU European consortia. My research develops model-free, decentralized charging strategies that respect realistic communication constraints, enable EVs to provide frequency ancillary services to TSOs without causing LV congestion, and models stochastic system dynamics using Monte Carlo simulations.

Chapter I

Research Pillars

01

Model-Free & Decentralized Smart Charging

Analyzing and developing model-free, decentralized charging strategies considering realistic communication between chargers and aggregators.

02

TSO Frequency Services without LV Issues

Allowing EVs to provide frequency ancillary services to transmission system operators (TSOs) without causing thermal overloads or voltage violations at the low-voltage level where most EVs are connected.

03

Monte Carlo Simulations

Assessing hosting capacity and charging dynamics via Monte Carlo simulations that consider the natural stochasticity of EV charging.
