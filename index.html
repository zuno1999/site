My Research in Brief

01. Model-Free & Decentralized Smart Charging

Analyzing and developing model-free, decentralized charging strategies that account for realistic communication constraints and delays between physical chargers and grid aggregators.

02. TSO Frequency Services without LV Grid Issues

Allowing EVs to provide frequency ancillary services to transmission system operators (TSOs) without causing thermal overloads or voltage violations at the low-voltage level where most EVs are connected.

03. Monte Carlo Grid Assessment

Assessing grid hosting capacity and control dynamics using Monte Carlo simulations that capture the natural stochasticity of EV user behavior, arrival times, and charging demand.
